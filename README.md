# The Bubble Cards

A modern pack after Thomas Bowles's 1720 South Sea Bubble playing cards: 52 cards and a Joker satirising the AI boom and its neighbours, each with a picture and a four-line epigram in the manner of the *Bubbler's Mirrour*.

Devis'd by W. G., the Verses by an Engine, the Pictures by another, MMXXVI.

Satire, not investment advice. Figures on the cards are approximate and drawn from public reports.
